FAQ

Questions? We have answers.

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Do I need any technical knowledge to use this? ▾
Not at all. We handle the entire setup — server provisioning, model installation, security configuration, and channel wiring. You just start chatting the day we hand it over.
Is this really private — how can I verify it? ▾
The entire stack (Ollama, OpenClaw, your LLM) is open-source and runs on your dedicated VPS. You have full root access and can verify every process running on the server yourself.
What's the difference between this and just using ChatGPT? ▾
ChatGPT processes your data on OpenAI's servers. Our solution runs entirely on your own private server — your data never leaves it. You also pay a flat monthly fee instead of per-message charges.
Can I switch AI models later? ▾
Absolutely. Since we use Ollama, swapping or adding models is straightforward. We can assist with model upgrades as part of your support plan.
Can the AI be trained on my own documents? ▾
Yes — the Enterprise plan includes RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) setup, which allows your AI to reference your internal documents, knowledge base, or product data when answering queries.
What happens if my server goes down? ▾
We monitor uptime and proactively address issues. Our VPS infrastructure is on high-availability nodes with a 99.9% uptime SLA. In the rare event of downtime, we restore service typically within minutes.
Do you offer a demo or trial? ▾
Yes! Book a free 15-minute demo call and we'll walk you through a live private AI setup. You can see exactly what you'll get before committing to any plan.
What if I need more power as I scale? ▾
Upgrading your VPS specification is easy and we'll handle the migration. You can move between plans at any time — and qualify for the 5% discount when upgrading to a higher tier.
